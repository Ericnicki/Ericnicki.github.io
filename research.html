Research

Learning from experience.
Adapting through memory.

I am interested in fundamental problems in embodied AI: how agents explore, learn from mistakes, and use memory and LLM guidance to acquire adaptable policies.

I enjoy digesting research papers, identifying the ideas that matter, and quickly building on them through implementation and controlled experiments.

Research interests

Agent exploration

Meta-learning exploration patterns that help agents learn how the world works and adapt to new situations or tasks.

LLM-guided policy learning

Using language-model guidance to support autonomous policy learning, with memory that carries experience into new situations or tasks.

Memory-based reinforcement learning

Learning and adapting to unfamiliar dynamics by retaining interaction history and improving across repeated attempts.

Latent-conditioned value learning

Exploring bootstrap latent variables that separate uncertainty over future conditions from condition-specific value predictions, with the aim of improving TD targets, critic gradients, and policy learning.

Research question

Latent-conditioned value learning

Can critics preserve distinct hypotheses about the future instead of averaging their implications into a single prediction? I’m interested in learning a posterior over bootstrap latent hypotheses alongside conditional critic and value functions. This would combine deterministic predictions conditioned on a hypothesis with uncertainty over which hypothesis applies, allowing different conditions to produce different TD targets.

I want to investigate whether this structure provides more useful gradients and improves the efficiency of critic and actor learning.
